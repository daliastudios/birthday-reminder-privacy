Privacy Policy

Birthday Reminder by Dalia Studios
Last updated: October 16, 2026

Dalia Studios ("we," "our," or "us") provides the Birthday Reminder mobile application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information You Enter

The app may let you enter names, birthdays, birth years, phone numbers, notes, favorites, and reminder preferences. This information can be personal, particularly when it concerns other people. Please only enter information you have permission to store.

2. Storage and Use of Information

Birthday information is designed to be stored locally on your device for the app's features, including displaying birthdays and preparing reminders. The app uses local device storage for this purpose. We do not operate a user account or a remote birthday database for this app.

Information may also be handled by your device's operating system or other apps when you choose features such as sharing, calling, messaging, or adding an event to your calendar.

3. Notifications

If enabled and permitted, the app can schedule local notifications about upcoming birthdays, such as one week before, one day before, and on the birthday. Notification delivery depends on your device settings and Android permissions.

4. Backup, Export, Import, and Sharing

Birthday Reminder stores birthday information and app settings locally on your device. Depending on your Android device, operating system version, and backup settings, Android's automatic backup or device-transfer features may include app data in system backups or transfers. These processes are managed by Android and your device's settings. Dalia Studios does not independently operate or receive these system backups.

If you use the app's own backup, export, import, or sharing features, the files you choose to create or share may contain birthday information and notes. You are responsible for choosing where those files are saved and with whom they are shared. Other apps or services you select may process shared information under their own privacy policies.

You can use the app's export and import features to back up or transfer birthday information yourself. Keep exported backup files secure and delete them from locations where you no longer want them stored.

5. Phone Calls, Messages, and Calendar

If you choose an available calling, messaging, or calendar feature, the app may open another app on your device or pass information needed for the action you requested. Calls, messages, and calendar entries are subject to your confirmation, device settings, and the privacy practices of the relevant service.

6. Gift Ideas

The app may display sample gift ideas for inspiration. Sample items and prices may be illustrative and are not necessarily offers for sale. If future versions include links to merchants, those merchants may collect information according to their own policies. Any material changes to our practices will be reflected in an updated policy.

7. Disclosure and Sale of Information

Dalia Studios does not sell personal information entered into the app. We do not maintain a remote birthday database. Information may be disclosed when you choose to share it through another app or service, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

8. Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it through available app features or remove the app or its data. Exported files and information shared with other services may remain in those locations until you delete them separately.

9. Security

We encourage you to protect your device and any exported backup files. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure. Access to locally stored information may depend on your device's security settings and backup configuration.

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to collect information directly from children. Users should take care when entering information about minors and should follow applicable laws and the requirements of their location.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact Dalia Studios at dalia.studios.support@gmail.com.
